# NGX Insight Field-to-Source Matrix v1

**Status:** Day 4 implementation mapping, updated 2026-11-18  
**Purpose:** Map the original Phase 1 metrics and approved MVP additions to their capture, verification, and provenance routes.  
**References:** [Data Contract v1](../data-contract-v1.md) and [Day 1 Bank Universe and Provider Symbol Crosswalk](../ngx-insight-day1-bank-universe-crosswalk.md)

## Scope and interpretation

This matrix retains the original 16 Phase 1 keys and adds four approved source-reported inputs (`total_assets`, `customer_deposits`, `gross_loans`, and `operating_income`) plus two conditional derived income-share displays. GTCO and Zenith Bank are the initial representative companies. Resolve each issuer through the crosswalk's stable `company_id`; do not use a provider symbol as the internal ID.

The crosswalk keeps core banks/banking groups (Cohort A) separate from mortgage banks (Cohort B). Include both cohorts in source-availability testing, but report results separately; do not combine them in peer statistics until comparability and metric eligibility are explicitly established. This matrix does not make every Financial Services company a bank. Cohort eligibility for comparisons follows the approved crosswalk and data-contract rules.

The target financial-data window is FY2021-FY2025. Capture an additional immediately preceding comparison period (for example, FY2020 for FY2021 growth) when required to represent the provider's published growth metric correctly. Record unavailable periods; do not infer or calculate missing growth values.

Planned source means the intended capture route, not confirmed endpoint or account coverage. The original 78-row GTCO/Zenith sample described in this document was a pre-Day-3 fixture and is not the current database snapshot. Day 3 registered all 16 issuers and imported audited-report observations plus manually captured TradingView market observations. The authoritative Day 3 totals and metric-to-screen adapter rules are recorded below; older source-discovery notes are retained as historical context where explicitly marked.

## Initial Company Analysis MVP metric scope

The Company Analysis hub and Section Detail mockup has four core sections: **The Business**, **Financial Health**, **Valuation**, and **Risks**. Quick Check is a separate two-position summary (key strength and worth watching), with a fuller breakdown screen; its positions must be supported by the evidence rules below or show the existing neutral insufficient-evidence state. The mapping-manual examples for Financial Health and Valuation are comparative metric pairs. The examples and mock values are illustrative, not evidence or approved thresholds.

“Essential” means included in the first metric-to-screen wiring when an eligible observation exists. It does not mean every issuer already has a value. Missing, conflicting, stale, or unverified observations remain visibly unavailable/insufficient and are never replaced with mock values.

### Derived income-share routes

`net_interest_income_share_of_operating_income` and `non_interest_income_share_of_operating_income` are conditional derived display measures, not provider fields. Their inputs are source-reported `net_interest_income`/`non_interest_income` and `operating_income`; formulas and reconciliation/alignment gates are defined in the data contract. Current evidence does not establish `non_interest_income` or a compatible operating-income denominator, so both derived measures are currently missing/unavailable for display. Never infer a component by subtraction without explicit support in the issuer's accounting presentation.

### Market-price route and quality gate

Kobo recent-price endpoint access is verified only for GTCO and Zenith on the tested Free plan, for seven recent records each. Supplied raw records include two GTCO and four Zenith OHLC consistency issues; preserve those values and verify them before use. This confirms endpoint access, not accurate prices, historical coverage, valuation-metric coverage, or access for other issuers. NGN Market GTCO financials (`PLAN_REQUIRED`, Business vs tested Free) and Kobo fundamentals/historical prices (Starter vs tested Free) are inaccessible through those tested routes on the current plans; their underlying data availability is unknown. No price-dependent insight may use the suspect records as a verified input.

## Day 3 starter banking rules and evidence states

These are candidates based on the approved metric priorities, not activated rules or claims that peer coverage exists. Comparisons describe relative observations and do not label high/low values automatically favourable or unfavourable. Use an eligible, separately reported core-bank peer group with aligned periods/dates, definitions, units, accounting scope and (for CAR) regulatory basis. The minimum peer count/coverage threshold is undecided; do not produce a sector average until it is approved and the required comparable data exists. For direct descriptive Business comparisons, larger scale or an income mix is not a strength by itself.

| Candidate / section | Beginner question; required keys and periods | Evidence and condition | `signal` | `no-signal` | `partial` | `insufficient` / missing-data treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `bank_income_composition` — The Business | How is reported income split between interest and non-interest sources? Derived share identifiers `net_interest_income_share_of_operating_income` and `non_interest_income_share_of_operating_income`; annual inputs for the same period. | Use the two contract formulas only after the source definitions/components reconcile against reported `operating_income`; compare only compatible issuer/peer observations. The output is descriptive, not a performance grade. | Reconciled shares and an eligible comparison support a factual description of the observed mix. | Complete comparable values are evaluated and the specific optional narrative condition is not met; retain the values without a conclusion. | One component/denominator or comparable period is available but the full share/comparison is not. | Missing inputs, unreconciled definitions/components, incompatible scope/period, or no eligible peer basis. Do not infer components or call a mix good/bad. |
| `bank_franchise_scale` — The Business | How large are the balance sheet, customer-funding base and lending book? `total_assets`, `customer_deposits`, `gross_loans`; same annual period. | Exact annual report definitions, Group/Bank scope and units; distinguish customer deposits from bank deposits and gross from net loans. Compare values descriptively only. | Complete comparable values support a factual size comparison. | Complete comparison is evaluated and no separate comparative narrative is selected; show underlying figures without a grade. | Some balances or peer-period values are absent/incompatible. | No aligned observations or definitions cannot be reconciled. Missing balances do not imply a small or weak franchise. |
| `bank_roe_vs_peers` and `bank_cost_to_income_vs_peers` — Financial Health | How do shareholder returns and operating efficiency compare? `roe` and `cost_to_income_ratio`, each with a compatible annual period and eligible peers. Separately display the `net_income` annual observation trend without calculating growth. | Require exact published ratio labels, period, scope, basis and compatible peer observations. Lower/higher direction is reported neutrally; only compare after each ratio's basis is aligned. | A complete comparison supports a stated relative observation (ROE above peer comparison or cost-to-income below it), without recommendation. | Complete comparable data shows the opposite/equal relation; state the relation neutrally. | Some annual values exist, but a bank/peer period, scope, basis or ratio is missing or incompatible. | No eligible published ratio or peer set. The sample's net-income observations may be displayed as a preliminary sequence only; do not calculate `net_income_growth`. |
| `bank_valuation_multiples_vs_peers` — Valuation | What market multiple is paid relative to earnings and book value? `pe_ratio`, `pb_ratio`, supporting `eps` and compatible book-value basis; same observation date. | Require published multiples, date, scope and stated EPS/book-value bases; compatible evidence across peers. Do not calculate missing multiples. | Complete comparison supports a factual statement that a multiple is above/below peers, not that the share is cheap or expensive. | Complete comparison shows equal/opposite relation; report without a value judgement. | A multiple exists but its date, basis, peer value or comparable input is incomplete. | No published multiple or no compatible date/basis/eligible peer group. Missing valuation data is not a warning. |
| `bank_credit_quality_vs_peers` — Risks | How does reported loan quality compare? `npl_ratio` with same reporting date, gross/net definition, scope and eligible peer set. | Use only exact published NPL ratios with compatible definitions. A lower/higher ratio is a relative fact, not a universal risk threshold. | Complete evidence supports a factual relative observation. | Complete evidence shows equal/opposite relation; report neutrally. | Some observations exist but definition/date/scope or peer set is incomplete. | No eligible NPL observation or peers. Missing NPL data is not evidence of poor credit quality. |
| `bank_capital_adequacy_vs_peers` — Risks | How does reported capital adequacy compare? `capital_adequacy_ratio` with same reporting date, scope and regulatory basis. | Preserve exact CAR type/regime and distinguish Group from Bank values. Do not compare incompatible regulatory definitions. | Complete compatible evidence supports a factual relative observation; do not equate higher with automatically safer. | Complete compatible evidence shows equal/opposite relation; report neutrally. | Some CAR observations exist but regulatory basis, period, scope or peer coverage is unresolved. | No eligible CAR comparison or incompatible regimes. Missing CAR evidence is not a risk warning. |

For every candidate, `signal` and `no-signal` require complete eligible evidence; `partial` means some usable inputs exist but a required pair/comparison is incomplete; `insufficient` means there is no valid basis to evaluate. Do not emit a company conclusion from `partial` or `insufficient`. For income-composition and scale descriptions, a signal means compatible values support a factual difference; no-signal means the complete compared values are identical. Evaluate ROE, cost-to-income, P/E, and P/B independently; a mixed result across metrics must not be collapsed into one state. For ratio comparisons, signal/no-signal report whether the stated relative relationship holds and carry no automatic positive/negative tone. No arbitrary numeric thresholds beyond the explicitly chosen peer comparison are introduced. Quick Check may surface a sourced outcome only; otherwise retain its explicit neutral insufficient-evidence state rather than filling a position.

## Day 4 database-to-screen adapter

The existing `companies` table supplies canonical `company_id` and `company_name`. Route slugs map to `company_id` in server-side display configuration; NGX symbols are display/source aliases only and are never used as database keys. `market_observations` is the current storage source for annual financial observations, market observations, and dividend-yield observations. There is no separate dividend or corporate-disclosure table in the current migration, so corporate disclosures are deferred until a source and storage model exist.

| Company Analysis section | Display group | Observation keys |
|---|---|---|
| The Business | Balance sheet | `total_assets`, `customer_deposits`, `gross_loans` |
| The Business | Lending position | `loans_to_deposits_ratio` |
| Financial Health | Profitability | `net_income`, `roe`, `roa` |
| Financial Health | Income generation | `net_interest_income`, `non_interest_income`, `operating_income` |
| Financial Health | Growth | `revenue_growth`, `net_income_growth` |
| Financial Health | Efficiency | `cost_to_income_ratio` |
| Valuation | Market price | `share_price` |
| Valuation | Valuation | `pe_ratio`, `pb_ratio`, `eps` |
| Valuation | Dividends | `dividend_yield` |
| Risks | Asset quality | `npl_ratio`, `loan_loss_coverage_ratio` |
| Risks | Capital strength | `capital_adequacy_ratio` |

These are display groupings within the existing four sections, not claims that the metrics share the same definition or are comparable. Display each eligible published observation with its own period, unit, source, and basis. Do not calculate missing values or comparison conclusions. Keep current and historical market periods distinct, including separate TTM, indicated, and fiscal-year dividend yields.

The adapter excludes ineligible values from metric display while retaining explicit status rows in historical coverage. Each displayed observation retains its canonical value/unit and exposes the exact source label, original source value/unit, period/date, source name/locator, basis, and verification status. Peer comparisons remain absent until comparable evidence supports them. Current-price reads use the latest eligible `share_price` observation by `company_id` and retain its observation date and source.

Historical financial display shows FY2021-FY2025 for each mapped annual metric. Canonical NGN and USD amounts use `m`, `b`, or `t` suffixes; the exact published value and source unit remain available in source details. A stored zero remains numeric zero. Periods without a usable value display an em dash; the underlying missing/unavailable status stays in the record rather than being shown as internal database wording. A fiscal year with no row is shown as a dash, not as issuer nondisclosure. Market observations retain their own periods (for example, `Dec 2025` and `Current`) and are not relabelled as fiscal-year results.

### Day 5 Quick Check rules

Quick Check evaluates only two source-backed criteria: latest eligible annual `net_income`, and latest eligible annual `capital_adequacy_ratio` when that same observation's recorded basis states a numeric minimum. Positive net income is marked as met; negative net income is flagged for attention; zero, absent, or ineligible net income is insufficient. CAR at or above the explicitly stated minimum is marked as met; CAR below it is flagged. When the source does not state a minimum, CAR remains insufficient. Each result links to the observation and shows its period, canonical and published values/units, source, basis, and verification status. These checks do not constitute a rating or investment recommendation; no general NPL, ROE, P/E, or other cutoffs are inferred.

## Capture and conflict rules

- For every capture, record the stable `company_id`, source/provider, exact source label, source locator, raw value/unit, canonical value/unit, financial period or market observation date, publication date when available, retrieval/capture timestamp, statement scope, basis, and capture method as required by the data contract.
- For an original audited-report transcription, this recorded source evidence is sufficient for `primary_source_checked`; a second-person or second-source review is optional and may be recorded as `independently_reconciled`.
- For TradingView, manually capture only a visible published value with its date and basis. Do not extrapolate historical annual figures from a chart or treat an undated current metric as an FY observation.
- Use an API value only when its response identifies the requested published metric and gives enough period/date, scope, and basis information to classify it. The crosswalk establishes symbol aliases; it does not establish metric endpoint coverage.
- If a source was checked but does not provide the field, mark it `unavailable`. If no eligible capture has been made, leave the value blank and mark it `missing`.
- When a later annual report's comparative differs from the audited report issued for that fiscal year, use the fiscal-year report unless the issuer explicitly identifies a restatement or correction. Record the later difference in the source note or basis; do not duplicate or silently overwrite the observation. Reserve `conflicting` for material disagreements that remain unresolved or incompatible definitions, units, periods, scopes, or bases.
- Do not calculate a substitute ratio or growth value, except for the two conditional income-share displays explicitly approved and defined in the data contract. Use published observations for all other metrics.
- Do not compute sector averages until comparable observations exist under the data contract's peer-eligibility rules.

## Day 5 peer comparison implementation

- Candidate peers are drawn only from the same approved cohort: core banking issuers are compared with core banking issuers, and mortgage banks with mortgage banks.
- A row is eligible only when both observations are available and sourced, and match on metric key, provider-published source label, reporting period, canonical unit, statement scope, and recorded basis. Current market observations also require the same observation date.
- The screen displays the latest compatible period per metric, with the exact published labels and source references for both issuers. It does not calculate sector averages, mark a side as better, or interpret the numerical difference.
- If no compatible sourced rows exist, the peer is omitted from the comparison list; a directly opened comparison route explains that evidence is insufficient.
- Current peer candidates are selected from the same cohort and ordered by the number of compatible metrics, showing up to three. This ordering describes coverage only, not issuer quality.
