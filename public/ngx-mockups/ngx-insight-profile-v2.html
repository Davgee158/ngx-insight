How Insight works.

Understand businesses
We break financial reports into plain language so you can understand how a company makes money and whether the business is improving.
Compare evidence
Instead of showing a number in isolation, we help you compare it with competitors and understand what it may indicate.
Record your thinking

Your decisions stay yours
No recommendations, no ratings, and no "Strong Buy" badges. We show the evidence so you can reach your own conclusion.
Why we built this
Many investing apps assume you already know how to evaluate a business, or rely heavily on analyst opinions. Insight was built to help people develop the confidence to make informed investment decisions using evidence they can understand.

Insight doesn't execute trades. Anything you buy or sell happens through your own broker — we help you decide, not transact.
