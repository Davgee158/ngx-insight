2. "How NGX Insight works" — the philosophy, stated plainly
Restructured from negative-first ("we don't...") to positive-first: what the app actually does every day (Understand businesses, Compare evidence, Record your thinking), with the "we don't recommend" content folded into the last principle rather than repeated four times. Marketing-sounding line cut and replaced with something plainer. New: "Why we built this" — one paragraph explaining the gap this product fills, not another restatement of philosophy.

How NGX Insight works.

Understand businesses
We break financial reports into plain language so you can understand how a company makes money and whether the business is improving.
Compare evidence
Instead of showing a number in isolation, we help you compare it with competitors and understand what it may indicate.
Record your thinking
Watchlists, portfolios, and your Decision Journal remember your own investment journey.
Your decisions stay yours
No recommendations, no ratings, and no "Strong Buy" badges. We show the evidence so you can reach your own conclusion.
Why we built this
Many investing apps assume you already know how to evaluate a business, or rely heavily on analyst opinions. NGX Insight was built to help people develop the confidence to make informed investment decisions using evidence they can understand.

NGX Insight doesn't execute trades. Anything you buy or sell happens through your own broker — we help you decide, not transact.
